## ✨ Feature Showcase: The Craftsmanship Behind Each Character

### 1. **Interactive Stroke-Order Forge**
Forget static animations. HanziFlow renders every stroke as a **vectorized brushstroke** that responds to your input speed and direction. The system tracks not just *whether* you drew the stroke, but *how* you drew it—providing granular feedback on starting angles, curvature, and proportional balance.

### 2. **Radical DNA Mapping**
Each character is broken down into its constituent radicals, displayed as a **color-coded genetic map**. This isn't just a list—it's an interactive diagram where clicking any radical reveals its own etymology, common variations, and how it morphs when combined with others.

### 3. **Adaptive Recall Lattice**
HanziFlow doesn't use a simple spaced-repetition algorithm. Instead, it constructs a **multidimensional lattice** across your learning history, weighing your performance by:
- Semantic context (do you confuse this character with a similar-meaning one?)
- Phonetic confusion (does the tone pattern trip you up?)
- Visual proximity (are you distorting proportions near stroke intersections?)

The lattice adjusts dynamically, interleaving review sessions that challenge your *specific* weak intersections rather than generic flashcard decks.

### 4. **Handwriting Recognition with Personality**
Our recognition engine is trained on **20,000+ real handwriting samples**, not just printed fonts. It discerns personal quirks—your natural slant, your press intensity—and distinguishes between "creative variation" and "incorrect structure."

### 5. **The Character Incubator** (🗄️ Experimental)
A completely novel feature: paste any Chinese text (article, poem, chat log), and HanziFlow will **incubate** unfamiliar characters by:
- Extracting them with frequency weighting
- Generating personalized mnemonic stories based on *your* interests (sports, science, cooking)
- Creating short, contextual dialogues that only use the characters you've already learned

## ⚙️ Technical Architecture

### The Glyph Rendering Pipeline
1. **Character Decomposition** — each glyph is parsed into structural components
2. **Vector Path Generation** — strokes are converted into Bézier curves
3. **Priority Layering** — visual opacity adjusts based on learning stage
4. **Haptic Synchronization** — mobile devices receive subtle vibration cues at stroke completion

### Data Storage & Privacy
Your learning data is stored **locally by default** (SQLite for desktop, IndexedDB for web). Cloud synchronization uses **end-to-end encryption** with zero-knowledge architecture—your learning journey belongs to you alone.

### Offline-First Design
The entire application functions **without any network connection** after initial installation. All etymological databases and recognition models are bundled locally. Updates are delivered as incremental patches, not full re-downloads.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### I'm a complete beginner. Will HanziFlow work for me?
Absolutely. The diagnostic only probes your *comfort zone*, then builds outward. There's no "too basic" or "too advanced"—the lattice adapts to your current intersection of knowledge and curiosity.

### Does it work with traditional characters?
Yes, HanziFlow supports **both simplified and traditional** scripts, individually or simultaneously. You can even set a "simplified-first but show traditional on hover" preference for reading flexibility.

### My writing looks nothing like the standard stroke order. Is that a problem?
HanziFlow distinguishes between **pedagogical standard** (what textbooks expect) and **personal expression** (what your hand naturally does). We teach the standard as a baseline, then help you develop your own consistent, readable style.

### Can I use HanziFlow alongside other learning apps?
You certainly can. We don't demand exclusivity. Our data export features make it easy to integrate your progress with other tools—HanziFlow complements, never confines.
